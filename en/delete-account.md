# Account and Data Deletion — Zsplit

**Last updated:** 17/09/2026

> This page explains how to delete your account in the **Zsplit** app (`com.zsplit.app`) and what happens to your data. The Portuguese version is the legally binding text: [/pt/excluir-conta](../pt/excluir-conta).

## How to delete your account

### Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

1. Open Zsplit and sign in.
2. Go to the **Perfil** (Profile) tab.
3. Tap **Excluir conta** (Delete account).
4. Confirm.

Deletion happens **immediately**, at no cost, with no waiting queue. It is **irreversible**.

### Option 2 — by email

If you can no longer access the app — for example you lost access to your sign-in email, or you already uninstalled it — send a request to:

**zplit.team@gmail.com**

Use "Account deletion" as the subject and include the **email address registered** on the account. We reply within **15 calendar days**, as required by the Brazilian data protection law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018, Art. 18). We may ask for additional confirmation before deleting, to make sure the request comes from the account holder.

## What is deleted

When the account is deleted, everything associated with it is removed **immediately, in cascade**:

- the account itself and its email address;
- your display name;
- the **events** you created;
- the **participants** in those events, including any phone numbers you entered;
- the **expenses**, splits and payments recorded between participants;
- your **receipt-import credit balance**;
- your **purchase records** for credit packs;
- your **receipt-import records**.

**Receipt photos** are not on this list because they are never stored: the image is sent for reading, the line items are extracted, and the image is discarded when processing ends.

---

## What is not deleted, and why

- **Infrastructure backups.** Supabase, which hosts the database, keeps short-lived backups for service continuity. Deleted data leaves those backups according to Supabase's retention policy.
- **Store records.** **Google Play** and the **App Store** keep their own records of purchase transactions under their own policies. Deleting your Zsplit account does not erase those, because they are not under our control. Requests about them go to the store directly.
- **Legal obligations.** Where the law requires a record to be kept, that obligation prevails for the period the law requires.

---

## Timing

Deletion from inside the app is **immediate**. Deletion requested by email happens within **15 calendar days** of the request.

There is no grace period and no way to recover an account once deleted.
